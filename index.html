What we do

Our services

Industrial sheds & warehouses

Pre-engineered steel structures for factories, godowns and workshops, designed for span, load and weather.

Roofing & sheeting

Metal roofing, polycarbonate and turbo ventilators for homes, terraces, car parks and commercial buildings.

Gates, grills & railings

Main gates, sliding and folding gates, window grills, staircase and balcony railings in MS and SS.

Civil construction

Residential and commercial buildings, extensions and renovation, from foundation to finishing.

Structural steel work

Mezzanine floors, staircases, beams, columns and trusses fabricated and erected on site.

Repairs & maintenance

Welding repairs, re-sheeting, rust treatment and painting to extend the life of existing structures.

Who we are

A workshop and site team you can rely on

Maruti's Sriram Fabrications & Constructions handles fabrication and construction under one roof. We take care of measurement, design, fabrication in our own workshop, and installation on site, so you deal with one team from start to finish.
